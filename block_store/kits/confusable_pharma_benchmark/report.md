### Schema

- **corpus:** `doc_id`, `title`, `text`, `metadata` (`active_ingredient`, `dosage_form`, `route`, `source_url`, `document_type`)
- **queries:** `query_id`, `text`, `metadata` (`expected_answer`, `target_ingredient`, `is_exclusive`)
- **qrels:** `query_id`, `doc_id`, `score`

### Quality Findings

- **Exact duplicates:** 0 across all JSONL files.
- **Near duplicates:** ~64.4% of corpus chunks have high 8-gram Jaccard overlap (≥0.9) with another chunk, driven by shared FDA guidance boilerplate (draft disclaimers, Office of Generic Drugs contact text).
- **ID integrity:** No duplicate `doc_id` or `query_id`; every `qrel.query_id` exists in `queries`; every `qrel.doc_id` exists in `corpus`; exactly one qrel per query.
- **Answer verification:** Expected answers are abstractive summaries generated by GPT-4o-mini and validated by a Claude 3 Haiku agent. After punctuation normalization, the majority are not verbatim substrings of their target chunk, but sampled answers are consistent with (paraphrased from) the source text. No contradictions were detected in the sampled subset.
- **Language:** English (`en`).
- **PII:** None detected; content is drug/product names and regulatory study requirements.

### Provenance & Licence

- **Publisher:** Ritivel Labs Inc. (dataset card and methodology report).
- **Original source:** U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) Product-Specific Guidance documents; each chunk includes a `source_url` to the originating FDA PDF.
- **Licence:** Apache-2.0 (stated in `README.md` dataset card).
- **Commercial use:** Allowed.
- **Redistribution:** Allowed.
- **Jurisdiction:** US (FDA).
- **Effective dates:** Document dates appear only in free text. Earliest observed: January 1999; latest observed: December 2025. No structured `effective_date` field is present.

### Synthetic Content

- **Corpus:** Derived from official FDA PSG PDFs (not synthetic).
- **Queries & expected answers:** Generated by GPT-4o-mini; exclusivity validated by Claude 3 Haiku on Amazon Bedrock.
- **Synthetic status:** `partial`.

## Verdict

**APPROVE_EVAL_CANDIDATE**

Approved for use as a retrieval evaluation candidate in the pharma domain. The one-to-one query-chunk mapping, included raw corpus, and permissive licence make it suitable for product retrieval testing. It should not be treated as golden ground truth because the answers are LLM-generated/validated abstractive summaries and the corpus contains substantial chunk-to-chunk boilerplate overlap.
