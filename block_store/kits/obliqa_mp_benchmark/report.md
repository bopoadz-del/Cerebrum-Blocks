## Dataset Inspection
- **Record count:** 2,976 QA pairs
  - Train: 2,083
  - Validation: 446
  - Test: 447
- **Schema per record:**
  - `QuestionID` (UUID string)
  - `Question` (string)
  - `Passages` (list of objects): `ID`, `DocumentID`, `PassageID`, `Passage`
- **Passage count distribution:** 2 passages (2,207), 3 (563), 4 (127), 5 (54), 6 (25)
- **Unique DocumentIDs in passages:** 37
- **Question lengths:** 77–329 characters (median 185)
- **Passage lengths:** 114–1,353 characters (median 632)
- **Duplicates:** 1 exact duplicate question pair (same text, different `QuestionID`, both in train). Near-duplicate rate (Jaccard ≥ 0.8) ≈ 0.5% of records.
- **Split integrity:** Zero `QuestionID` overlap between train/validation/test.
- **Language:** English; only 73 non-ASCII characters across all questions.
- **Synthetic status:** Majority synthetic. Questions and answers were originally generated by GPT-4; ObliQA-MP is a filtered multi-passage subset. The HuggingFace dataset is tagged `synthetic-data` and the README describes it as a synthetic benchmark.
- **PII:** No personal data identified. Seven generic ADGM/FSRA contact emails (e.g., `monitoring@adgm.com`) appear in passages; these are public regulatory contact addresses, not individual PII.

## Licence and Provenance
- **Dataset publisher:** RegNLP research group (Tuba Gokhan, Ted Briscoe).
- **Paper:** *Grounded Answers from Multi-Passage Regulations: Learning-to-Rank for Regulatory RAG*, NLLP 2025 / ACL.
- **Dataset licence:** Apache-2.0 (stated in HuggingFace dataset card).
- **Commercial use:** Allowed under Apache-2.0.
- **Redistribution:** Allowed under Apache-2.0.
- **Underlying source:** Abu Dhabi Global Markets (ADGM) public financial regulations. The original structured corpus is distributed in `RegNLP/ObliQADataset` as `StructuredRegulatoryDocuments.zip`. ADGM’s public website/app terms reserve intellectual property rights; reuse of the raw regulatory text is not explicitly open-licensed, though the RegNLP authors have released the derived QA benchmark under Apache-2.0.

## Hard-failure gates
No hard failures triggered.
- **Licence:** Apache-2.0 is known and permissive.
- **Synthetic content:** Explicitly disclosed as synthetic; not presented as authoritative legal advice.
- **PII:** None beyond public regulatory contact emails.
- **Corruption:** No malformed files among downloaded splits.
- **Jurisdiction/version:** Jurisdiction known, but version dates are absent from the released QA files — flagged as a weakness, not a hard rejection.

## Verdict
**APPROVE_EVAL_CANDIDATE**

ObliQA-MP is approved as a candidate reasoning/retrieval evaluation set. It is a well-structured, permissively licensed, multi-passage regulatory QA benchmark with clean splits. It is not approved as a raw RAG corpus because it contains only question-passage pairs, not a full continuous corpus. The score reflects the lack of verified answers, the absence of negative-evidence cases in the released artifact, and missing embedded jurisdiction dates.
